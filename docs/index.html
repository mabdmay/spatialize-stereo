    Where the speaker is

    Drag to move it. Green is in front of you, amber is behind. The blue cone is the way you are facing.

    Motion lock

    Keeps the speaker anchored in the room as you turn, the way Head Tracked mode does on AirPods. Safari can only read the phone's sensors, so turn the handset with you — true AirPods head tracking needs the native app in /ios.

    Off — the rig turns with you.

    Placement

    Room

    Drop Effect amount to zero to A/B against the untouched file.

    Use headphones

    The whole effect is binaural. Over laptop or phone speakers the two ears mix in the air and it collapses to ordinary stereo. Any wired or Bluetooth headphones work — this does not need AirPods.

    Turn Apple's own Spatial Audio off for your headphones while using this, or the two renderers stack.

    Runs entirely on device. No file you choose ever leaves the phone.
